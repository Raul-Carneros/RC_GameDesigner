Transformers: Earthspark - Expedition

3D Action Game · Tessera Studios for Outright Games · PC, PS, Xbox, Switch

Play as Bumblebee in a narrative-driven adventure of drifting, driving and fighting, chasing Dr. Meridian, "Mandroid", in the hunt for ancient technology.

Role: System Designer & Level Designer

The challenge

Deliver a licensed action game that reads instantly as Transformers: traversal that flips between vehicle and robot must feel seamless, and combat has to stay readable for a young audience without losing depth.

What I worked on

  • Design and balancing of core combat and the drive/fight transform loop in Unreal Engine 5.
  • Blocked out and shipped 3 levels: layout, pacing and encounter logic, from greybox to playable.
  • Implemented features in Blueprints, iterating directly in-engine with the team.
  • Wrote GDDs, pitches, flowcharts and narrative docs that kept design aligned.

Impact

Shipped on PC, PlayStation, Xbox and Switch as the official game of the animated series.

  • GDD
  • System diagrams
  • Level blockouts
  • Combat balancing

Iron Jaw

Isometric Roguelike · PC, Steam · 2022

Choose one of five mechs, pick your weapons and enter the world of Bulian to wipe out the bug swarms. Endure as long as you can.

Role: System Designer

The challenge

A build-driven roguelike lives or dies on variety: five playable mechs and a pool of weapons have to feel meaningfully different while staying balanced across endless runs.

What I worked on

  • Design, implementation and balancing of combat mechanics and systems in Unity 3D, alongside the team.
  • C# programming for game systems and mechanics.
  • GDDs, pitches, flowcharts and narrative documents.

Impact

Shipped on Steam with five playable mechs and build-driven combat.
